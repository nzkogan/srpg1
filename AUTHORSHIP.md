# Authorship and provenance notes

A short, factual map of who made what in this repository, kept so the project can answer questions about it later (store disclosure, copyright, contributor rules). It is a working note, not legal advice, and the project's owner should correct it.

- **The world, the rules and the decisions** live in `canon.xlsx` -- the owner's workbook. It holds the setting, the sects, the cast, the chapter structure and the decisions marked as authored (the Hierophant's sect, the Enlightened One's sect, the roster, the permadeath/epilogue premise, and so on).
- **Rows marked DRAFTED, ADDENDUM or "first-draft quality"** in `canon.xlsx` were drafted by an AI model working to the owner's direction (for example the support chains, the weapon, mount and forge catalogues, and the numeric tuning); each such note says what is a guess. Anything not so marked is the owner's.
- **The code** (`game/`, the build and validation scripts) was written by an AI model (Claude, via Claude Code) at the owner's direction, session by session. Commits carry a `Co-Authored-By` and `Claude-Session` trailer. Tests accompany the systems.
- **The design notes** (`*_DESIGN.md`) were written by the AI model to record what was built from canon and which choices were its own guesses ("Drafted guesses to confirm"); they are the audit trail for those choices.
- **Not used:** no code, art or text from other games, no decompiled or ported material. Enemy, weapon and creature names are drawn from the setting's own inspirations (real-world mythology and history, as canon notes); the game's pictures are blockout placeholders.
- **If this ever ships:** check the current disclosure rules of the storefront for AI-assisted content, and have the authorship of the AI-written parts reviewed by someone qualified.
